### 1. Peptide Brand
- **Slug:** `peptide-brand`
- **Tag:** E-commerce
- **Filters:** E-commerce
- **Location:** National
- **Engagement date:** December 2024 to present
- **Card summary:** Launched, scaled and rebuilt WooCommerce research peptide storefronts in a category that Meta, TikTok, Mailchimp, Klaviyo and even WooCommerce's own extensions refuse to serve.
- **Hero metrics (card and detail page):**
  - `$230K` / Revenue in a single month on $70K in ad spend
  - `2.83x` / Google Performance Max ROAS across 956 conversions
- **Stat tiles (detail page):**
  - `3.3x` / Return on total ad spend in the peak month
  - `$146,820` / Google Ads conversion value in 59 days
  - `7` / WooCommerce extensions replaced in under 3 weeks
  - `24%` / Share of revenue from a new affiliate channel in its first 30 days
- **Services (card):** PPC, Conversion Tracking, E-commerce Operations, Email, Affiliate Program

**The Challenge**
Research peptides sit in one of the most restricted ad categories online. In the first months the brand lost one platform after another: WooCommerce's parent company dropped support for its shipping, product feed and automation extensions; Mailchimp and Klaviyo closed its email accounts; the Meta ad account was disabled; and TikTok suspended the account the day its first ad went live. Later, a payment processor shut off and conversion tracking stopped reporting sales to the ad platforms. The business had to keep selling through all of it.

**Our Approach**

01. Launch on the channels that stay open
We set up analytics, Google Ads, Merchant Center, Meta, Instagram and X from scratch in December 2024, then put the budget behind Google Performance Max once it proved it could serve the catalog. As ROAS held, we scaled daily spend to $1,000 and then $1,500. At its peak the store did $230K in revenue in one month on $70K in ad spend.

02. Rebuild the stack when platforms walk away
When the WooCommerce extensions were pulled with a February 22 deadline, we sourced and installed replacements for all seven (UPS and USPS shipping, the Google product feed, cart recovery automation and more) before the cutoff. When Mailchimp and Klaviyo blocked the account, we moved email onto a self-hosted system the business controls.

03. Tracking that survives change
Payment processors changed often, and each change broke purchase tracking. We rebuilt it server side, so every paid order reports to Google Ads, Microsoft Ads and X no matter which processor took the payment. A nightly offline upload recovered $2,315 in Google-driven revenue in its first week that the ad account had never seen. We also fixed the product feed itself: categories mapped to "Hardware" and missing brand and identifier fields on every product.

04. Channels no platform can shut off
We built an affiliate program with a tracked link, partner terms and commission tiers. Partners are paid only on real sales, and in its first 30 days the program produced 24% of revenue at zero media cost. On X, a same-day, same-audience test showed that adding a discount line cut cost per click from $1.14 to $0.86, and X's algorithm then put 2.5 times the spend behind that version.

**Services delivered:** Google Ads (Performance Max, Shopping) · Merchant Center · X Ads · Server-side Conversion Tracking · WooCommerce Operations · Email · Affiliate Program · Ad Policy Strategy
